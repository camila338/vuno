## Usage

<ul>
  <li><code>leading</code> with <code>back</code> on pushed screens and with <code>close</code> in modal flows.</li>
  <li>Titles of one to three words; if they don't fit, they truncate with an ellipsis.</li>
  <li>At most two actions on the end side, with <code>IconButton</code>.</li>
  <li>In a flow, the title can be replaced by a <code>StepProgress</code>.</li>
</ul>

## Accessibility

<ul>
  <li>The title is announced as a heading in VoiceOver and TalkBack.</li>
  <li>Back and close have labels (“Back”, “Close”) and are 48.</li>
  <li>On Android, the system Back button or gesture does the same as the arrow: never block it.</li>
</ul>
