## Usage

<ul>
  <li>Amounts sit on the end side with tabular figures and a sign; incoming ones use <code>valueTone="positive"</code>.</li>
  <li>The detail is one line of context (date, category, number).</li>
  <li>With <code>onPress</code>, the row shows a chevron.</li>
  <li>Rows grow with their text: never fixed heights.</li>
</ul>

## Accessibility

<ul>
  <li>A tappable row is announced as a button with its title and detail.</li>
  <li>A minimum height of 48 (<code>list.row.height</code>).</li>
</ul>

## Do's and Don'ts

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="The sign and color tell the direction of money."><Live width={300}><List><ListRow icon="arrow-down" title="Title" detail="Detail" value="+$120.00" valueTone="positive" /></List></Live></Do>
  <Dont caption="Without a sign or detail it's unclear whether money came in or went out."><Live width={300}><List><ListRow title="Title" value="$120.00" /></List></Live></Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do caption="A one-line detail."><Live width={300}><List><ListRow title="Title" detail="Oct 2 · Groceries" /></List></Live></Do>
