## Usage

<ul>
  <li>3 to 4 per row. With more, group them in a menu.</li>
  <li>The row distributes the space; the component sizes to its content.</li>
  <li>Labels of one or two words.</li>
</ul>

## Accessibility

<ul>
  <li>The label is part of the touch target and is the name VoiceOver and TalkBack read.</li>
  <li>The circle is 56 (24 icon + <code>action.inset</code>), above the 48 minimum.</li>
</ul>
