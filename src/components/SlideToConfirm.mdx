## Usage

<ul>
  <li>Only for the last step of a flow that moves money. Everywhere else, <code>Button</code>.</li>
  <li>The label is the verb of what happens: “Slide to send”.</li>
  <li>Released early, it returns with <code>motion.spring.soft</code>; on confirm, a success haptic.</li>
</ul>

## Accessibility

<ul>
  <li>No gesture with a screen reader: a VoiceOver or TalkBack double tap triggers the “activate” action.</li>
  <li>The thumb is 48 (<code>slide-confirm.thumb.size</code>).</li>
</ul>
