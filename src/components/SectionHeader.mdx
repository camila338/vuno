## Usage

<ul>
  <li>With an icon only in product sections, inside their <code>VerticalProvider</code>.</li>
  <li>The detail explains the section in the person's words.</li>
  <li>At the end, a status or a tertiary link (“See all”); never the main action.</li>
</ul>

## Accessibility

<ul>
  <li>The title is announced as a heading: VoiceOver (rotor) and TalkBack (heading navigation) jump from section to section.</li>
</ul>
