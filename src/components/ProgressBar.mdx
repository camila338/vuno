## Usage

<ul>
  <li>Progress in <code>on-accent</code> and the track in <code>accent</code> (or <code>surface.card</code> on an accent card).</li>
  <li>Always pair it with the figure or percentage as text.</li>
  <li><code>slim</code> for lists and tight spaces.</li>
</ul>

## Accessibility

<ul>
  <li>With Reduce Motion it appears filled, without animation.</li>
  <li>The figure as text is what VoiceOver and TalkBack read.</li>
</ul>
