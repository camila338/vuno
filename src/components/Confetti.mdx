## Usage

<ul>
  <li>Only for positive milestones the person triggers, such as creating or reaching a goal. Never when paying or signing in.</li>
  <li>Once per moment; it doesn't replay when returning to the screen.</li>
  <li>It goes with the highlight chip and the success haptic.</li>
  <li>Its duration (<code>confetti.duration</code> → <code>motion.duration.effect</code>), gravity and number of pieces come from component tokens.</li>
</ul>

## Accessibility

<ul>
  <li>With Reduce Motion it doesn't play.</li>
  <li>Decorative: hidden from VoiceOver and TalkBack, and it never blocks taps (<code>pointerEvents="none"</code>).</li>
</ul>
