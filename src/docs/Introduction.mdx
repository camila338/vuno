## Principles

Five principles guide every interface decision. They come from the brand brief and apply to every vertical.

<Grid min={300}>
  <LinkCard title="1 · One clear action per screen" icon="flagOutline">
    Put the next step first. One purple button per screen; context appears when it's needed.
  </LinkCard>
  <LinkCard title="2 · Hierarchy before decoration" icon="trendingUpOutline">
    Balances, statuses and decisions read at a glance. Big headlines and figures, a calm interface.
  </LinkCard>
  <LinkCard title="3 · Shared components" icon="copyOutline">
    The same patterns, radius, spacing and icons in Banking, Save, Invest and Credit. The vertical is context.
  </LinkCard>
  <LinkCard title="4 · Color with meaning" icon="colorFillOutline">
    Color guides and is never the only signal of a state. Every text pair meets WCAG AA.
  </LinkCard>
  <LinkCard title="5 · Motion with purpose" icon="pulseOutline">
    Short transitions that confirm, explain or connect an action. A soft bounce, never decoration at rest.
  </LinkCard>
</Grid>
