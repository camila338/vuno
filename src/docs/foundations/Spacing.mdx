## Usage

- Balanced density: home and brand screens use `space.gap.section`; transactions and details use `space.gap.list`.
- The screen side margin is `space.inset.screen` (20) in every vertical.
- At least `space.xs` (8) between two touch targets.
- Never a loose value: if a space is missing, propose a token (see Contributing).
