## The 70 / 20 / 10 split

70% of the interface is `ink` neutrals. 20% is the purple action and the vertical accents. 10% is lime, reserved for milestone moments.

## Primitives

Ten families in steps `100`–`900`; `500` is the base of each. A dot (●) marks the steps the semantic layer uses. The interface never uses a primitive directly.

## Usage

- `color.brand.primary` is only for the primary action and focus. One purple button per screen.
- `color.brand.logo` (#6C63FF, Vuno Purple from the brief) is for the logo and text-free brand surfaces: with text it doesn't reach AA.
- Credit uses the purple ramp (`purple.300` / `purple.900`, 10.23:1). Its accent shares a step with the disabled primary button; the button also carries `opacity.disabled`, so they don't read alike.
- A vertical color identifies the vertical and nothing else; it never means success or status.
- When an accent is the background, its content uses the matching `on-accent`.
- No pure white or black: the lightest surface is `ink.100` and the darkest `ink.900`.
- `coral` stays in the palette as a brief color but has no semantic role.
