## Usage

- Cards use `border.default` and no shadow.
- `elevation.floating` for the tab bar and floating buttons; `elevation.overlay` for sheets, modals and menus.
- The scrim behind a sheet is `color.surface.scrim` with `opacity.overlay`, never pure black.
- `border.default` is decorative. Fields and controls use `border.input` (3:1).
