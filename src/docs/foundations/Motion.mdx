## Durations

Two kinds of duration, kept apart on purpose.

- **Transitions** (`press` → `celebrate`, 100–400 ms) move the interface from one state to another. The person waits for them, so they stay short. For reference, Material 3's whole transition scale ends at 1000 ms.
- **Effects** (`motion.duration.effect`, 1800 ms) are decorative and never block the interface: you can keep tapping while they play. They live outside the transition scale, so a long effect never becomes a precedent for a slow transition.

## Usage

- On iOS, a pressed element scales to 0.96 for `motion.duration.press` and springs back with `motion.spring.soft`. Android uses the Material ripple instead (see Platforms).
- Entrances use `motion.easing.standard`; exits use `motion.easing.exit` with `motion.duration.state`.
- `motion.spring.bounce` only in 10% moments (see the Milestone moment pattern).
- Navigation uses each platform's native transitions.
- With Reduce Motion on, springs become an instant change or a `motion.duration.state` fade, and effects such as confetti don't play.
