## Usage

- Buttons, chips, the tab bar and icon buttons use `radius.pill`.
- Cards use `radius.card` (20), fields `radius.input` (16) and sheets `radius.sheet` (28) on the top corners.
- A nested element has a radius smaller than or equal to its container's.
